Lullin & Ferrari

a digital rebranding for LULLIN & FERRARI, an art gallery in Zurich. The motion identity is based on the idea of a free layout, giving the artsworks space to breathe and lay the focus on the art itself. For the motion, a playful and dynamic path animation is used to uphold the idea of a free layout. Project realised with Cristina Bortolas.

logo

Following the spirit of the free layout, we created and coded our own logo tool. The logo has several variations and is customizable, but still remains recognizable.
