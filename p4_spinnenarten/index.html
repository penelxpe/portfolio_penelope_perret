Welche Spinne spinnt wie?

The exhibition "In the Spider's Web" takes the form of a walk-through spider web, connecting three main stations that range from stories and knowledge to self-reflection. The goal is to offer a new perspective on a frequently misunderstood creature. At the second station, "Which spider spins what kind of web?", visitors learn about the various types of webs found in the spider world. An animation accompanied by audio is projected onto a central platform. Surrounding it are six different spider specimens, each of which lights up whenever its specific type of web is mentioned in the animation.
