3D Animation /sound design

LVF Festival

A teaser in form of a 3D-Animation Video for the Live Video Fest, a festival across the germanic part of Switzerland. A project realized in collaboration with Guillherme José Pires Nunes. The teaser was used for the festival's social media channels and as a trailer for the festival itself. It was also projected there in real time.
